Places to Explore

From world-famous monuments to art and peaceful gardens, Paris has something for every visitor.

Here are three well-known places to add to a Paris sightseeing list.

🗼

Eiffel Tower

One of the most recognisable landmarks in the world. Visitors can enjoy views across the city.

🖼️

The Louvre Museum

A famous art museum with a large collection spanning many periods and cultures.

⛪

Notre-Dame Cathedral

A historic cathedral on the Île de la Cité, known for its Gothic architecture.

🌳

Luxembourg Gardens

A green public garden where visitors can take a relaxing break from sightseeing.

🎨

Montmartre

A neighbourhood known for its artistic history, streets and views of Paris.

🚤

River Seine

Walk along the riverbanks and admire bridges and landmarks from the water.

Visitor tip: Check official attraction websites for current opening times, ticket rules and access information before travelling.
