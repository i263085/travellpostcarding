Paris Travel Guide

A few practical ideas to help make your visit comfortable and enjoyable.

🚇

Getting around

Paris has a public transport network including the Metro, buses and trains. Check routes and ticket options before your journey.

🌦️

What to pack

Check the forecast before leaving. Comfortable walking shoes and a light layer can be useful when exploring the city.

🥖

Food to try

Paris is known for bakery treats, pastries, crêpes and a wide variety of cafés and restaurants.

💶

Money

France uses the Euro (€). Check exchange rates and your payment provider's fees before travelling.

Before you go

  • Check passport, visa and travel requirements for your situation.
  • Book popular attractions in advance when required.
  • Keep an eye on personal belongings in busy places.
  • Learn a few polite French phrases, such as “Bonjour” (hello) and “Merci” (thank you).
  • Confirm transport schedules and local information close to your travel date.
